AI Ready Leaders

Four interactive modules that help leaders lead AI change with confidence: making the case, deciding what to hand to AI, coaching through AI mistakes and checking AI-drafted work.

Designed and built with AI as a development partner. Harbourline is a fictional company I created, so you can see how I'd tailor learning to a real client.

Senior and new leaders4 modules, 3 to 5 minutes eachPhone or laptop
  1. Module 1 Leading the AI Shift Your COO wants 30% cost savings from AI. Make three decisions that balance business value, your team's trust and risk. Branching scenario, 4 min
  2. Module 2 The Delegation Edge Sort eight leadership tasks: keep it human, let AI assist, or hand it to AI. Build a streak by matching expert calls. Judgement sort, 3 min
  3. Module 3 Conversations That Count Coach a strong analyst whose AI shortcut put client data at risk, without losing her trust. Conversation practice, 5 min
  4. Module 4 Trust, But Verify Review an AI-drafted email and catch the five problems before your name goes on it. Review challenge, 3 min

About these modules

Every module uses realistic scenarios, expert feedback on each decision, and gamification such as XP, levels and badges. Progress is saved in your browser, and a programme passport fills in as you complete each module.

Accessibility

The modules work with a keyboard, respect reduced-motion settings and use labels as well as colour to show results.
