Trust, But Verify

Reviewing AI drafts before your name goes on them

Scenario: check before you send. You asked Harbourline's AI assistant to draft an email announcing a new on-call rota. It took ten seconds and reads well. But your name is at the bottom.

Drafted with AI
Hi team, from 1 March we're moving to a four-week on-call rota. Research shows rotating rotas improve productivity by 47%. …

🗺️How this works

  1. Read the AI-drafted email as if you're about to send it to your team.
  2. Tap any sentence you'd fix before sending. Tap it again to unflag it.
  3. When you're happy with your review, select Check my review.
  4. Each problem caught earns 40 XP and each fine sentence flagged costs 10. A perfect review earns a 50 XP bonus.

Tip. Five sentences have problems. Read slowly: AI writing is fluent, and fluency can hide errors.
