Coaching your team through AI mistakes without losing trust

Scenario: the quiet shortcut. You're three months into your first management role at Harbourline. Priya, one of your strongest analysts, sends a polished client report. Reading closely, you spot two statistics that aren't in the client's data.

🗺️How this works

  1. Read the background, then step into a 20-minute one-to-one with Priya.
  2. At three moments in the conversation, choose what you'd say.
  3. See Priya's reply and a coach's note, and watch trust and clarity shift.
  4. Finish with how the conversation landed, plus your XP, rank and badges.

Tip. Pick the response closest to what you'd naturally say under pressure. Press A, B or C to choose.

Three habits for new leaders

  1. Open with curiosity, not accusation. You'll get the real story faster.
  2. Separate the tool from the standard. Using AI isn't the issue; unchecked facts and leaked data are.
  3. Turn one mistake into a team norm, ideally co-written with the person who made it.
